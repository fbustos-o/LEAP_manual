# And Operator

## Syntax

Expression AND Expression

See also: [Operators](Operators.md), [IF](If.md), [True](True.md), [False](False.md), FnAnd, Or, Not, Xor

## Summary

The And operator returns [True](True.md) if both the left hand term and the right hand term are True.  Otherwise it returns [False](False.md).

## Examples

IF(2=2 and 3=3, 10, 5) = 10  
IF(True and False, 10, 5) = 5
