# LaggedValue

## Syntax

LaggedValue(Years) or  
LaggedValue(Branch:Variable, Years)

## Summary

Calculates the value of either the current branchAn item on the tree. Different types of branches are represented by different icons on the tree or of another branch lagged by the specified number of years .
