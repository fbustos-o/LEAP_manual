# Efficiency

See also: [Analysis View](../02%20-%20Views/Data_View.md), [Demand Analysis](Demand.md), [Useful Energy Demand Analysis](Useful_Energy_Analysis.md), [Coproduct Efficiency](Coproduct_Efficiency.md)

Efficiency is the percentage annual average thermal efficiency of a demand device defined as 100 * useful energy delivered / final energy consumed. In order to be able to specify efficiency, make sure your end-use branch is configured as a category with aggregate energy intensity branch type (![](../assets/images/NewButtons/Tree/FolderGreen.png)) and is set to conduct a [useful energy demand analysis](Useful_Energy_Analysis.md). Efficiency can then be entered for any of the technology branches (![](../assets/images/NewButtons/Tree/Technology%20Green.png)), below this branch.

NB: Efficiency data are only visible for technology branches when conducting a [Useful Energy Demand Analysis](Useful_Energy_Analysis.md).
