# Coproduct Efficiency

See also: [Analysis View](../02%20-%20Views/Data_View.md), [Demand Analysis](Demand.md), [Useful Energy Demand Analysis](Useful_Energy_Analysis.md)

The efficiency of production of a co-product fuel (energy carrier).  Efficiency data are only visible for those technology branches that are part of a [Useful Energy Demand Analysis](Useful_Energy_Analysis.md).  These branches are indicated by green technology icons (![](../assets/images/NewButtons/Tech%20Green.png)).  In addition to specifying the fuels consumed by these technologies, you can also specify that one or more of these technologies also produce a co-product.  You can do this by editing the properties of the technology as shown below.

![](../assets/images/NewScreens/CoProduct%20Efficiency.png)

For example, you might specify an industrial combined heat and power system that is designed to meet a heating demand by consuming natural gas, but which also produces electricity as a co-product.

Note that in most systems the sum of the efficiency and co-product efficiency terms should be less than or equal to 100%.
