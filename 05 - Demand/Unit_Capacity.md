# Unit Capacity

See also: [Analysis View](../02%20-%20Views/Data_View.md) , [Demand Analysis](Demand.md)

Used when conducting full energy system optimization modeling and only available at [optimized technology branches](Optimize_Devices.md) (![](../assets/images/NewButtons/Tree/Technology%20Green.png)). Use this variable to specify the capacity of each individual device in power units. (e.g. in kW or BTU/Hour). Numbers of devices are calculated during energy system optimization calculations based on the overall level of energy demand, the load shape, and the unit capacity of each device. Look at result variable Demand: Demand Devices to see the total number of devices in each year at each demand branch.
