# Marginal Final Energy Intensity

See also: [Analysis View](../02%20-%20Views/Data_View.md) , [Demand Analysis](Demand.md) , [Stock Analysis](Stock_Analysis.md), [Usage Factor](Usage_Factor.md)

Marginal final energy intensity is the annual final energy consumption per device for a new device introduced in a given year.  When using the stock analysis method, you will also specify a degradation profile for each device (in Current Accounts) to indicate how the energy intensity changes as devices get older.  LEAP will internally calculate the average energy intensity of all devices across all vintages.

## Units

LEAP lets you enter energy intensities for technologies in energy, mass or volume units. It will also automatically convert your data from one unit to another. Note that when the fuel is a pure energy form such as electricity, the units must be an energy unit.
