# Capital Cost

See also: [Analysis View](../02%20-%20Views/Data_View.md) , [Demand Analysis](Demand.md)

Used when conducting full energy system optimization modeling and only available at [optimized technology branches](Optimize_Devices.md) (![](../assets/images/NewButtons/Tree/Technology%20Green.png)). Use this variable to specify the capital cost per device of each optimized demand technology. Specified in currency units per device.  Costs are annualized based on the lifetime of each device. Default value is 0.
