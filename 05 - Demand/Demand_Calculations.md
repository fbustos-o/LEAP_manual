# Demand Calculations

See also: [Demand Analysis](Demand.md)

Energy demand calculations in LEAP differ depending on whether you are conducting an Activity Analysis, a Stock Analysis or a Transport Analysis. In addition, within an activity analysis, they differ depending on whether you are conducting a final or useful energy demand analysis on a set of branches.

For more information, see:

* [Activity Analysis Calculations](Activity_Analysis_Calculations.md)
* [Transport Analysis Calculations](Vintaging_Calculations.md)
* [Stock Analysis Calculations](Stock_Analysis_Calculations.md)
