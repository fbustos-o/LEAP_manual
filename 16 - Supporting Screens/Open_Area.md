# Open Area

Menu Option: Area:Open

Use the Open Area option to open a new area to view or edit. In LEAP, you always work on one Area at a time. An Area is typically a country, a region or some other bounded energy system that you wish to study in LEAP. An area contains one set of Current Accounts data (the data describing the base year of your study) and any number of alternative future scenarios.
