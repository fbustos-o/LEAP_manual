# Final Energy Demand

**See also:** [Results View](../02%20-%20Views/Results_Screen.md), [Useful Energy Demand](Useful_Energy_Demand.md)  
Result Type: Demand

Shows the final energy consumed by end users, such as households, industry, transport and agriculture. Excludes energy use in the Transformation sectors. Results can be shown organized by branch, fuel, scenario, year and region (in multi-regional areas).

Final energy demand is the amount of fuel (or heat or electricity) consumed in demand technologies.  The fraction of final energy that actually does useful work  in providing an energy service (heating, cooling, transport, etc.) is the [useful energy demand](Useful_Energy_Demand.md).
