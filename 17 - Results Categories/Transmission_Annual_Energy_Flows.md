# Transmission Annual Energy Flows

**See also:**  [Transmission Energy Flow](Transmission_Energy_Flow.md),  [Transmission Power Flow](Transmission_Power_Flow.md), [Transmission Capacity](Transmission_Capacity.md)  
**Result Type:** Transmission

Net annual energy flow through a transmission line.  See also the [vtransmissionannual](https://sei-international.github.io/NemoMod.jl/stable/variables/#vtransmissionbyline) variable in NEMO.  Only visible in scenarios where both transmission modeling and full energy system optimization are enabled and where a model has multiple regions.
