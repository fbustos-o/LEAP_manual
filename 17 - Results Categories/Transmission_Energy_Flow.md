# Transmission Energy Flow

**See also:** [Transmission Annual Energy Flows](Transmission_Annual_Energy_Flows.md),  [Transmission Power Flow](Transmission_Power_Flow.md), [Transmission Capacity](Transmission_Capacity.md)  
**Result Type:** Transmission

Energy flow through a transmission line in each time slice.  See also the [vtransmissionbyline](https://sei-international.github.io/NemoMod.jl/stable/variables/#vtransmissionbyline) variable in NEMO.  Only visible in scenarios where both transmission modeling and full energy system optimization are enabled and where a model has multiple regions.
