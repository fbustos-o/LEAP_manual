# Transmission Capacity

**See also:** [Transmission Annual Energy Flows](Transmission_Annual_Energy_Flows.md), [Transmission Energy Flow](Transmission_Energy_Flow.md),  [Transmission Power Flow](Transmission_Power_Flow.md)  
**Result Type:** Transmission

The amount of capacity built for each transmission line in a given year.  Only visible in scenarios where both transmission modeling and full energy system optimization are enabled and where a model has multiple regions.
