# Inputs

See also: [Results View](../02%20-%20Views/Results_Screen.md)  
Result Type: Transformation

This results category displays the annual energy consumed by a given Transformation module including both feedstock fuels used and auxiliary fuel use. Results can be shown organized by branch, scenario, year, fuel and region (in multi-regional areas).  Results can also be divided into feedstock vs. auxiliary fuel use.
