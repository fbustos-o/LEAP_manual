# Externality Costs

See
also: [Analysis
View](../02%20-%20Views/Data_View.md), [Cost-Benefit
Calculations](../01%20-%20Introduction/Transformation_Cost_Calculations.md)

Externality
Costs are social damage costs specified per unit of pollutant.
If you specify an externality cost for an effect, LEAP will multiply this
unit cost by the total emissions of that effect in each year of each scenario
to yield an overall externality cost of each pollutant. This cost will
be incorporated into LEAP's overall [social
cost-benefit calculations](../01%20-%20Introduction/Transformation_Cost_Calculations.md).
