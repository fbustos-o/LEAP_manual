# Non Energy Sector Effect Cost

See also: [Non Energy Sector Effect Analysis](Non-Energy_Sector_Analysis.md), [Non Energy Sector Effect Loading](Non-Energy_Sector_Analysis.md)

Use this variable to specify the total cost associated with measures to mitigate non energy sector effect loadings.

These costs will be included in LEAP's overall cost-benefit analysis, which can be viewed via the [Cost-Benefit View](../02%20-%20Views/Cost_Summary_Report.md).
