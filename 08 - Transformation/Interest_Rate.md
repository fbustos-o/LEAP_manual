# Interest Rate

See
also: [Analysis
View](../02%20-%20Views/Data_View.md), [Transformation Analysis](Transformation.md)

The real interest rate in percentage points used to annualize Transformation
Capital costs, using a standard [capital recovery factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_recovery_factor)
(CRF).  Using the default CRF method, capital costs are spread forward
into equal real payments every year for each year of the lifetime of a
process.  If any costs occurring in years after the end year are
ignored. For more information see [Costs](Capital_Costs.md).
