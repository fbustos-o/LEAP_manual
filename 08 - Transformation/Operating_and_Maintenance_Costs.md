# Operating & Maintenance Costs

See
also: [Analysis
View](../02%20-%20Views/Data_View.md), [Transformation Analysis](Transformation.md),
[Cost
Calculations](../01%20-%20Introduction/Transformation_Cost_Calculations.md)

Note:
Cost data is shown only if the Cost
check box is checked in the [Module
Properties](Module_Properties_Wiziard.md) screen and if costs are enabled on the Scope tab of the
[Settings](../16%20-%20Supporting%20Screens/Basic_Parameters_Screen.md)
screen.

You can enter both fixed and variableData that can change over time. operating and maintenance (O&M)
costs. Fixed O&M costs are incurred regardless of the energy produced
by a process, and are entered per unit of capacity (e.g. per MW). Variable
O&M costs are entered per unit of energy outputs, inputs or losses.
Note that if a moduleA Transformation branch representing an energy industry or sector, such as electricity generation, oil refining, district heating, charcoal production, or electricity transmission and distribution
does not contain capacity data, then you will only be able to enter variable
O&M costs to represent the combined annualized capital and O&M
costs of each process.
