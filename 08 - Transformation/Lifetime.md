# Lifetime

See also: [Analysis View](../02%20-%20Views/Data_View.md), [Transformation Analysis](Transformation.md)

The life (in years) of a process.  This variable is used to calculate when processes added under the endogenous capacity tab will be retired and replaced, thus incurring additional capital costs.  Capacity specified on the exogenous capacity tab is NOT automatically retired. The lifetime variable is also the period used when amortizing Transformation capital costs. For more information see [Costs](Capital_Costs.md).
